Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

gws is a private command-line tool. It runs entirely on the local machine of its single author and is used only to access that author own Google account.

What data is accessed

With the author explicit consent, gws reads and writes data in the author own Google account: Gmail messages, Calendar events, Drive files, and Contacts.

Where data goes

Data is not sent to any third party, analytics service, or external server. There is no backend, no telemetry, and no data sharing. Requests go directly from the local machine to Google APIs.

What is stored

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the author machine in the .config/gws directory. No Google user data is persisted beyond what the tool caches locally in order to function.

Third parties

None. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email registered on the Google Cloud project for this application.
